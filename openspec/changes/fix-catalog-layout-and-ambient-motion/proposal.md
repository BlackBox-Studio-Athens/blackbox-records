# Repair public catalog layout and ambient motion

## Why

Disintegration wraps a lone final letter on Home, the Afterwise backdrop contains abrupt concert lighting, and Releases changes from catalog tiles to featured panels after its live offers arrive. Featured artwork also stretches into a tall empty frame.

## What Changes

- Fit Home film titles to their available width while keeping short titles at the approved size.
- Replace the prepared Afterwise background with calm footage of the band actively playing, using longer performance excerpts, gentle transitions and a matching poster; retain a still if no suitable footage exists.
- Render editorial Releases roles before hydration and keep them stable while live offers update truthful actions and badges.
- Keep featured artwork square, complete and unstretched across desktop and mobile.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `release-catalog-presentation`: editorial roles and geometry are stable from SSR through offer resolution; buying eligibility remains live and independent.

## Impact

Web editorial/store components, Releases page markup, shared CSS, prepared Afterwise MP4/poster and focused unit/browser checks. No new dependency, Worker authority change, catalog mutation or release operation.
