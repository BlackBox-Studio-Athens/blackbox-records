## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Privacy disclosure distinguishes retention policy from implemented cleanup

Approved privacy information MUST identify retention periods or concrete determination criteria separately for order/payment/fulfillment records, withdrawal/return/support records, service inquiries, newsletter consent and suppression, availability alerts, technical logs/analytics, and browser cart storage. It MUST NOT promise automated erasure that the implementation and provider configuration do not establish.

#### Scenario: A fixed period cannot be established from source

- **WHEN** a category depends on unresolved work, statutory duties, claims, or provider settings
- **THEN** the draft identifies the relevant trigger and determination criteria
- **AND** unresolved owner/accountant facts remain explicit publication acceptance gaps without invented fixed dates.

#### Scenario: A cleanup path is implemented

- **WHEN** the notice describes browser-cart or availability-request removal
- **THEN** its scope and trigger match source behavior
- **AND** it does not imply that provider delivery records or legally retained transaction records are erased at the same time.

### Requirement: Privacy purposes and transfer disclosures follow evidenced roles

Approved privacy wording MUST identify applicable purposes and lawful bases, including technical analytics where used, and distinguish processor services from providers' separate-controller purposes. Transfers and safeguards MUST reflect evidenced provider arrangements without asserting EEA-only storage, accepted agreements or certifications without evidence.

#### Scenario: Provider documentation establishes SCC mechanisms

- **WHEN** Resend, Cloudflare or Stripe terms describe international-transfer mechanisms
- **THEN** the notice links the relevant terms and offers an appropriate contact path for safeguard information
- **AND** acceptance separately records account-specific agreement/assessment gaps.

#### Scenario: Consent functionality is being revised independently

- **WHEN** newsletter or one-off notification wording depends on a sibling implementation
- **THEN** only behavior demonstrated by that integration is described as available
- **AND** this change does not create sibling routes or infer that cookie-free analytics is automatically exempt from applicable law.
