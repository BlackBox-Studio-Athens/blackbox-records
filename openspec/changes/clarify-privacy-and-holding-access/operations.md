# Minimal privacy operating procedure

Decision date: 14 October 2026. The owner delegated the remaining routine decisions to Codex and requested the simplest adequate local solution. This procedure replaces the request for the owner to design a policy. It is an internal operating instruction, not public copy, evidence of past practice, or proof that a scheduled job exists.

## Use one contact and one private record

Use the existing published privacy contact, `orders@blackboxrecordsathens.com`. The store operator remains accountable; an assistant may prepare the work but cannot assume the controller's legal identity or silently send a response. Do not add a DPO title, new inbox, ticketing service, consent platform or paid tool.

Keep a small private case log alongside the existing restricted business records. One row per case is enough: opaque reference, received date, request type, due date, systems checked, decision/reason, action date and next review. Do not put customer addresses, identity documents, supplier invoices or provider credentials in Git. No example row represents a real case.

When a request arrives, verify identity proportionately using the existing conversation/order reference. Do not routinely request identity documents. For access, correction, erasure, restriction or objection, identify the relevant records and exemptions, then prepare a response within one calendar month. Any justified extension and its reason must be communicated within that first month. An erasure request does not authorize deletion of legally required transaction evidence or another person's data. Newsletter/availability controls continue to use the implemented verified capabilities.

For a suspected personal-data breach, first contain access and preserve minimal incident evidence. Record when the operator became aware, affected data/people, consequences and mitigation. Assess notification to the Hellenic DPA within 72 hours of awareness where required, and communication to affected people where the high-risk threshold applies. Do not automatically report every technical error or send notices without reviewing the facts.

## One monthly review, plus case closure

Review at case closure and once per calendar month while operating, with the first inventory/review due by 14 November 2026. This is a selected manual procedure, not an automation or a claim that historical reviews took place. No bulk deletion is enabled by this document. Apply the following rules record by record; retain only what has a current purpose, documented legal duty or specific claim/hold.

| Records                                       | Simple rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Orders, payment references, delivery, refunds | Keep what is needed for the open transaction. Once closed, separate required accounting/claim evidence from unnecessary contact/operational copies. Record the applicable duty and expiry/start event before erasing the required evidence. The statutory period is not inferred from the source or from the planned company. Review unresolved legal retention decisions each month; uncertainty is not an indefinite-retention policy. |
| Returns, complaints, withdrawal declarations  | Keep the active case and the minimal evidence needed for a remaining claim/duty. Remove unrelated copies after closure at the next review. A legal hold names the case, reason and next review; it does not freeze every record.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Service inquiries that do not become work     | Close the inquiry when the discussion ends. At the next review, remove unnecessary personal data unless a specific follow-up or dispute remains. If it becomes work, transfer only the needed material to that contract's records. Do not add the sender to marketing.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Newsletter contacts and consent               | Use the implemented mailbox-confirmation flow. Withdrawal/suppression prevents further marketing; do not erase suppression in a way that permits accidental re-subscription. Keep only the consent/withdrawal evidence needed for its identified purpose and review it separately from message logs.                                                                                                                                     |
| Availability requests and capability records  | Preserve the existing code's expiry, successful/final-failure removal and withdrawal rules. ## Analytics: technical intent is not legal clearance

The current intended use is aggregate readership/performance analysis, without adding cross-site profiles, marketing audiences or customer identifiers. The benefit is prioritising site reliability and content improvements. Restricted staff aggregate reports and data minimisation reduce exposure. Consent, objection handling, retention, actual beacon collection and the less intrusive alternative of not collecting analytics must still be assessed together.

This is a preliminary purpose/necessity/balancing record, not a completed legitimate-interest assessment. Cookie-free behavior alone does not establish an exemption from terminal-access rules. Under the owner's delegated choice, defer optional automatic beacon collection using `no-transform` on public gateway HTML/304 replies, preserving all existing cache and privacy directives. This is the same documented mechanism already used by Holding, without a banner, replacement tracker or provider-account mutation. Existing staff historical aggregate reads remain available. Item 6 is unchanged.

The code is prepared locally; it changes no live collection until separately released. Verify the proxied response and absence of manual/automatic beacon requests at that release. No manual beacon was found in current public source. Do not claim the current hosted site has stopped collecting, or that this header removes previously collected data. Align public wording with the actual released state before publishing it. [Cloudflare's FAQ](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/faq/) documents the automatic-injection mechanism and its limitation; ordinary edge/security measurements are separate.

## Sources and limits

- [GDPR Articles 5, 12, 13, 17, 28, 33, 34 and Chapter V](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng/): necessity, transparency, rights, providers and breach handling. Monthly review is our chosen workflow, not a statutory retention period.
- [EDPB Guidelines 2/2023, final version](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-22023-technical-scope-art-53-eprivacy-directive_en): terminal-access analysis is not limited to cookies.
- [Cloudflare customer DPA](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/); existing `handover.md` records the Resend/Stripe sources and Greek DPA evidence.
- [Hellenic DPA breach guidance](https://www.dpa.gr/foreis/asfaleia_dedomenwn/gnwstopoiisi_paraviasis) and [EDPB rights guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/respect-individuals-rights_en).

No actual retention sweep, account agreement, provider setting, rights response, breach notification or live content publication is claimed. The procedure resolves the routine policy choice. Missing external facts remain evidence gaps, not another owner questionnaire and not completed compliance.
