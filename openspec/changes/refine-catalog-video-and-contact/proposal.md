## Why

BlackBox releases currently also appear in Distro because of an earlier deliberate faceting rule. The user now wants that separation restored. Disintegration needs the same film-backed Home preorder presentation as LOTUS using the supplied Afterwise performance. Mobile footer links wrap awkwardly, and Services lacks a demo-sharing option.

## What Changes

- Exclude release-sourced Store Items from Distro through the shared classifier, preserving All, BlackBox Releases and canonical commerce identity.
- Keep Distro's mixed-format band ordering, filters and ordinary navigation.
- Prepare a lightweight silent Afterwise film and poster and recognize its exact YouTube ID through the existing showcase mapping. The user handles PRD clip metadata and publication.
- Balance mobile footer navigation without isolating Delivery information.
- Render Staff footer text as one flowing paragraph, without forced sentence breaks.
- Keep Home preorders visible outside Greece and show the existing international email-order notice.
- Add Share your demo to the existing Services inquiry flow and existing server-owned info inbox.
- Preserve announced vinyl independently of digital availability on Releases, including Anarchotribal before preorder opens.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `store-catalog-categories`: BlackBox releases are excluded from Distro.
- `distro-format-discovery`: mixed-format band ordering remains deterministic without BlackBox promotion.
- `store-preorders`: Afterwise uses the existing native backdrop and explicit full-video experience; international visitors can browse the same preorders.
- `app-shell-and-player`: mobile footer navigation remains aligned and accessible, with naturally wrapping Staff text.
- `services-inquiry`: demo links use the supported existing inquiry flow.
- `release-catalog-presentation`: announced vinyl is not mislabeled Sold out because its digital album is released or its offer is unavailable.
- `staff-workspace`: date/stage guidance explains the independent digital and physical timelines.

## Impact

Shared frontend classification/tests, showcase media mapping/assets, footer CSS, Services form/page and inquiry validation/routing/contract. Work happens directly on main and preserves the preceding uncommitted mobile/shipping change and unrelated Stripe migration. No new dependency, CMS schema, media hosting service, upload form or shipping/stock authority is introduced. Software release and user-owned PRD content publication remain separate acceptance steps.
