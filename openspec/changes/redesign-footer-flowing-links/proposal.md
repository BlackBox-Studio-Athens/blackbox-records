# Proposal

## Why

The owner approved a compact Flowing links footer and a factual disclosure of generative AI assistance. The current four-column phone footer cannot accommodate that approved composition without conflicting layout and height rules.

## What Changes

- Replace the footer composition with a responsive identity row, flowing destination links and copyright/disclosure area.
- Keep existing CMS copy, configured destinations, conditional privacy information, social assets and persistent shell behavior.
- Replace obsolete four-column/200px assertions with approved responsive size budgets and accessibility checks.
- Supersede the pending mobile-layout-rhythm footer prescription while preserving its historical evidence.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `app-shell-and-player`: compact footer layout, AI assistance disclosure and navigation continuity.

## Impact

The storefront-catalog footer and shared stylesheet, existing footer browser checks, DESIGN.md and related OpenSpec records. No new dependency, client script, public API, schema or route. Codex owns this explicitly approved implementation. Deployment is outside this change's local acceptance.
