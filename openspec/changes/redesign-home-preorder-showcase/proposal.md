# Proposal

## Why

The Home pre-order selector gives the official video less prominence than the approved cinematic reference. The user wants the film to fill a dedicated release scene after the existing Home introduction, with a centered identity and a strong ordering action, while preserving BlackBox's aesthetic and an equally considered no-video version.

## What Changes

- Replace the selected-video layout with a large cinematic scene using the authorized local Sidus video as a small silent native-video backdrop, centered release identity, truthful status and price, pre-order link, and actual artwork.
- Reveal subsequent pre-orders through normal scrolling above News, without redesigning the existing Home introduction. Keep a single sleeve in each release; expand only the full player and Close action after explicit Watch.
- Give no-video releases their own artist-photo/artwork composition, retaining Listen, metadata, purchase facts and monochrome treatment.
- Preserve live commerce authority, accepted publication data, release/ship-date distinctions, shell navigation, explicit full-video intent and reduced-motion/low-data fallbacks.
- Apply the approved Base sweep, subtle accepted artist links, noninteractive sleeves and arrow-only chapter navigation. Keep video shipping information beside its purchase action and the delivery-information link on its own line with clear space below the action.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `store-preorders`: Home presentation supports cinematic original-file video and purposeful no-video release scenes while retaining current buying and lifecycle rules.

## Impact

Public web Home showcase component, showcase JSON/candidate contract, scoped Home styles, small static media derivatives, relevant unit and browser tests, and design documentation. No checkout, stock, order, publication-authority or backend behavior change; no new runtime dependency. Local preview and source-bound validation are required. Hosted release is outside this request.
