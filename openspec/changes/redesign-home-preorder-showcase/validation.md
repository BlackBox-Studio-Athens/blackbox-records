### Checkout hold timing research

The current checkout flow requests a 35-minute expiry and binds the hold to the expiry returned by Stripe. Stripe's automatic `expires_at` accepts 30 minutes to 24 hours. A 15-minute policy needs a server-owned deadline that does not extend on retries, an independent timed call to expire an open Checkout Session, and idempotent hold release only after confirmed closure/nonpayment. Paid or processing payments remain protected; missing provider references require review. Browser countdowns and D1 date filters cannot safely release stock by themselves. Background scheduling/provider failures can delay closure, so a hard universal 15-minute release guarantee would be misleading. This is research only: no expiry constant, scheduler, provider mutation or cleanup policy was changed.

Primary sources: [Stripe limited-inventory guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/checkout/managing-limited-inventory.md?payment-ui=stripe-hosted), [manual session expiration](https://docs.stripe.com/api/checkout/sessions/expire), and [delayed payment fulfillment](https://docs.stripe.com/checkout/fulfillment.md?payment-ui=stripe-hosted).
