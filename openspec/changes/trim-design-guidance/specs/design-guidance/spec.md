## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Public design guidance describes current approved rules

DESIGN.md SHALL organize the public site's current visual rules by shared role, component and surface. Superseded instructions SHALL NOT remain actionable. Approved responsive, language, motion, accessibility and commerce-state exceptions SHALL remain explicit or link to their precise current owner.

#### Scenario: An agent reads a surface rule

- **WHEN** an agent reads Store, Releases or Home guidance
- **THEN** defaults and applicable exceptions identify the current approved treatment without reconstructing earlier iterations
- **AND** existing application behavior and design choices are preserved.

### Requirement: Design guidance routes to existing owners

Product strategy, Staff patterns, operational rules and research SHALL remain with their existing owners. Unique current rules SHALL be preserved before removing a duplicate or misplaced section.

#### Scenario: Staff guidance is removed from the public contract

- **WHEN** DESIGN.md replaces Staff detail with owner links
- **THEN** current chart/comparison details remain available in the backoffice reference
- **AND** publication and product rules are not duplicated as a second authority.
