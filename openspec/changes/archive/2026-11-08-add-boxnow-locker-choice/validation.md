Review round on Local at `6a474d58c04932059e1c7582d71e8681231abbd5`; the worktree also contains other uncommitted changes. The owner approved the visible checkout, Staff, email and finder result. These findings need an owner decision because their proposed fixes change that result.

- **R1, high — map fallback:** `BoxNowLockerMap.tsx` treats the iframe load event as map readiness. A frame that loads but never offers a usable selection will not trigger the 10-second fallback described in `design.md`. The existing browser test covers a hanging frame and the manual fallback, not this case. Decide how to detect readiness without closing a working map while the shopper is choosing.
- **R2, medium — shipping locker:** `paid-order-templates.ts` falls back to the shopper's chosen locker when the dispatch locker is null. Staff may clear both locker-used fields, so the email can name a locker that was not recorded as used. A direct template invocation confirmed this. Decide whether to omit that row when the locker used is unknown.
- **R3, low — Copy all:** `SendParcelDetails.tsx` displays the parcel size outside the copied rows. Copy all therefore omits it, and the displayed size has no individual Copy action, contrary to the Staff spec. Decide whether to change the approved Staff and clipboard result.

This also fixed real shipping emails losing the postcode that the approved preview shows. R3 keeps the screen; the Staff spec now says the suggested size is shown, not copied, because BOX NOW's form chooses it with a picker.
