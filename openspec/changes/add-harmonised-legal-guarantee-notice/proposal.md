# Proposal

## Why

The 14 October 2026 audit identified that existing legal-guarantee wording does not supply the separate harmonised notice required from 27 September 2026. Buyers need a prominent path to the prescribed official notice before purchase without changing checkout authority or implying a commercial durability guarantee.

## What Changes

- Prepare the official EU/Greek legal basis, approved-artwork source and a focused implementation handover for Claude Desktop.
- Specify a shop-level legal-guarantee reminder that reveals the full unmodified RGB notice on first activation, with readable accessible content and a direct Your Europe information link.
- Preserve the existing published terms and real seller/controller information. No fixture replacement, CMS save or publication is part of this preparation.
- Leave every UI source edit, including displayed text, to Claude Desktop under the user's superseding instruction. Claude must present a concrete preview for approval before non-text UI implementation.
- Keep GARAN absent unless separately verified producer evidence establishes the qualifying commercial guarantee. No commercial guarantee is asserted here.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `shopper-purchase-information`: a prominent harmonised legal-guarantee notice is available before purchase, separate from contractual terms and commercial guarantees.

## Impact

This delivery changes OpenSpec documentation only. Future UI work touches existing Store/checkout/terms presentation and locally served official artwork. No backend API, D1/R2 schema, provider configuration, dependency, tax setting, order-confirmation email, checkout gate or launch state changes are required. Content Publication and Software Release remain separately authorized operations.

See [handover.md](handover.md) for Claude's exact edit points and [legal-research.md](legal-research.md) for official evidence and limits.
