## Verified legal basis

- [Directive (EU) 2024/825](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/825/oj) article 2(3)(b) replaces Consumer Rights Directive article 6(1)(l): a prominent pre-contractual reminder of the legal guarantee and its main elements uses the harmonised notice. Article 2(5) introduces article 22a. Article 4 sets transposition by 27 March 2026 and application from 27 September 2026.
- [Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1960](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2025/1960/oj), article 1 and Annex I prescribe the notice; article 3 applies it from 27 September 2026. Annex I prohibits editing notice elements and requires RGB colour online. A4 minimum is the physical-display rule, not a web CSS-pixel minimum. The full text was also inspected through the [Spanish official gazette's EU-law reproduction](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=DOUE-L-2025-81437), because EUR-Lex direct requests returned a JavaScript challenge.

## Display guidance and official artwork

[Commission practical guidelines and files](https://commission.europa.eu/publications/practical-guidelines-and-high-resolution-vector-files-eu-notice-and-label-product-guarantees_en) provide official notices in all EU languages as PDF, PNG/JPG and SVG, with colour and print-only monochrome versions. Obtain RGB English and Greek originals there. Use the notice, not the GARAN label package. Keep the entire artwork, proportions, colours, typography and QR code intact.

[April 2026 practical guidelines](https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/3e4dbee6-8184-4256-923d-c52551c7e9f0_en?filename=Practical+guidelines+Harmonised+Label%26Notice+product+guarantees_ARES+27042026.pdf), section 2.3, PDF pages 17–22, describe online readability and a clickable equivalent of the QR destination. Illustrated first-click reminders appear on catalogue, header and checkout surfaces. These are service guidance, expressly not an authoritative interpretation by the Court. They support the compact proposal; the regulation itself expressly discusses GARAN nested display separately. Do not invent a mandatory notice on every product, mandatory new checkbox, or precise web pixel size.

The guidance also recommends the notice in confirmation email. That is recorded for the parent's separate future durable-confirmation work; it is not implemented or accepted here.

Official information destinations: [English guarantee rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/guarantees), [Greek guarantee rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/εγγυήσεις). The artwork's QR and the accessible click link must agree. The [Your Europe business explanation](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/consumer-contracts-guarantees/eu-legal-guarantee-notice-and-garan-label/index_en.htm) confirms the general website reminder and colour requirement.

## Legal notice versus GARAN

The notice explains existing statutory rights; it grants no new BlackBox commercial warranty. GARAN concerns a producer's separately offered, free durability guarantee covering the whole good for more than two years, when that information is available to the trader. Offering such a guarantee is voluntary; label use becomes required when the qualifying conditions apply. No qualifying guarantee evidence was supplied for this scope. Do not display GARAN or claim extra years by inference.
