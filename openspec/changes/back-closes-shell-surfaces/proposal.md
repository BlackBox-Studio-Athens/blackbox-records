## Why

Back currently leaves the page while Store Crate, Artists or Home full video is open, and closing detail overlays loses the listing offset. Browsing history must preserve the shopper's place and dismiss the top surface first.

## What Changes

- Preserve detail-overlay background scroll through open, nested navigation and close.
- Extend the existing marked same-URL history mechanism to Crate, Artists and full video, including Forward and consuming entries on explicit close.
- Check other public dialogs and add focused unit and browser regression coverage.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `app-shell-and-player`: surface Back/Forward and scroll/focus continuity.
- `module-boundaries`: provide the existing browser history helpers from web-platform to shell and Store.

## Impact

Public shell, Store interactions, shared browser history helper and focused tests. No commerce authority, native anchor behavior, publication or deployment changes.
