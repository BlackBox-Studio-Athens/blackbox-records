When the player is minimized

The same recording gets the same state everywhere. Amber means an existing player session, whether Bandcamp is playing or paused. Click another record to preview switching the active session.

Where Button Click does
The active record in Store or Releases Open player, amber border and quiet tint. Option C keeps its icon and gains the same border plus an accessible Open player label. Reopens the same player. Does not restart the track.
Other records Listen, neutral frame. Opens that recording and replaces the existing session.
The minimized player Player Ready, recording title, Open player and Stop. Open restores it; Stop ends the session and resets matching buttons.

Motion: two short cycles on hover/focus and when a session becomes active, then static bars. No continuous meter or “Playing” claim while playback is unknown. Button widths remain fixed when the label changes.

Recommendation: A is the closest refinement. B protects the artwork but makes each card 52px taller. C is compact but depends on an icon and tooltip. Releases retains its existing placement and larger primary button.
